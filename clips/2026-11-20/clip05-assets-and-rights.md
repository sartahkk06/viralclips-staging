Facts on screen:
- Every console version targets 30 FPS; there is no 60 FPS mode.
- PS5 Pro: 1440p internal, PSSR upscale to near-4K.
- Base PS5: 1440p, with a worst case of 23 FPS in heavy action.
- Series S: 720p with FSR 4 to about 1080p, often below 30.
- Frame time: 1000/30 ≈ 33.3 ms and 1000/60 ≈ 16.7 ms (arithmetic).
